SER010
-

The interpolated-string RESP writer: `RespContext`, `RespRequestBuilder`, `RespRequestFrame`,
`RespFragment` and friends, and the command groups built on them (`db.Strings`, `db.Hashes`, ...).

This is an experiment. The shape of the API, and whether it ships at all, is not settled; expect it to
change. It is exposed publicly so that it can be *used* and argued with, not because it is stable.

If you are extending the client with commands of your own, [Extending the client](../Extending) is the
guide; it covers what is stable today (`ExecuteResp`) as well as what is behind this switch.

Design notes, including what has been measured and what has only been reasoned about, are in
`design/interpolated-resp-writer.md`.

To opt in, add `SER010` to `NoWarn`, or suppress it at the call site.
